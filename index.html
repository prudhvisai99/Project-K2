SF4 → TSMC N02 functional cell mapping

Every functional standard cell instantiated in quasar_top — 6,356 cell types across 447 function roots, 1,635,084,961 flat instances — resolved to its TSMC N02 equivalent. SF4 names teal, N02 names plum. Click any cell name to copy it.

How to use this page

  1. Looking up one function? Stay on By function — 447 rows, one per SF4 root, with the drive range and Vt flavours present in the design. This is the day-to-day lookup.
  2. Need the exact instance name? Switch to By cell — all 6,356 full cell names with their constructed N02 counterpart.
  3. Always check the confidence chip. exact is safe to substitute. likely needs a datasheet check. approx and gap need a truth-table or pin-level review before you rely on them.
  4. Fill, decap, antenna and macros are deliberately excluded — they are inserted by the target technology's ChipFinish flow, not ported. This page is the port scope, not the whole netlist.

Three things that bite

Why approx is the largest band: most of it is not a wrong function, it is an SF4 construct N02 has no form for. The mapping stays functionally correct and loses the intent.

SF4-only cell modifiers

29.7 % of instances

MV1…MV8 M1-variants (16.7 %), PV placement-variable (7.2 %) and the POK pin-optimization prefix (5.6 %) resolve to the plain base function. Pin-access and placement intent — the reason those cells exist — is lost. Expect different routability and congestion.

Gate-length axis disappears

24 % on C54L06

SF4 ships C54L04 and C54L06 (biased Lg) as separate cells. N02 has a single 3 nm gate length, so the L06 leakage/speed lever is gone. N02's per-cell LLKG suffix is the nearest replacement mechanism.

Functions with no N02 cell

293 types

The AO/OA deep ladder (AO2222 AO221 AO222 AO31 OA31 OA221) does not exist in N02 and needs resynthesis. Fused AOI22NAND3/4 and OAI22NOR3/4 must decompose into two gates. N02 has no negative-edge scan flop, so ~378 K SDFFN* instances have no target.

Vt retarget

SF4's four Vt flavours rank-map onto N02's. This is an ordering by threshold, not an electrical equivalence — absolute Vt and leakage differ between nodes. N02's lvtll and ulvtll low-leakage flavours are unreachable from an SF4 netlist.

The mapping

Scope. Functional standard cells only — combinational, sequential, clock, tie, spare, level shifter. Excluded: FILLSGCAP*/ECOFILLSGCAP* fill and decap (38 types, 1.489 B instances), ANTENNA (2 types), and 119 hard macros. Those are inserted or instantiated against the target technology, not translated cell-for-cell.

Inputs. Cell list and flat counts from the Pegasus LVS census of quasar_top (run pv_run_eco1_08062026), verified against the LVS-reported flat device count. Function mapping from SF4_to_N02_stdcell_mapping.tsv (125 rows). SF4 geometry from SF4LPP_20260424_H200_H254/S6P25T/FE-Common/LEF. N02 cell names from the n02-docs MCP server (tcbn02p_bwph130pnpnl3p48cpd_*, v110a/110b).

Before you substitute anything: N02 area for a given function is not proportional to SF4 area, drive strengths do not correspond numerically (a SF4 D2 ≠ an N02 D2), and the Vt mapping is a rank correspondence. Size by timing.
